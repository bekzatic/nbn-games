About us

We're building NBN Games for players, not for a grade

NBN Games is a small team that grew out of a shared passion for games. We're building the store we'd actually want to use: fast, honest about pricing, and free of noise.

Our principles

  • Players first

    Every product decision is checked against one question: does this make things easier for a real player.

  • Price transparency

    No hidden markups — the price on the shelf always matches the price at checkout.

  • Always improving

    NBN Games is a student project we keep refining with every iteration.
